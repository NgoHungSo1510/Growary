## Review Focus

- **Concurrent double-purchase on last stock (`stock: 1`) or tight coin balance:** Two simultaneous `POST /api/rewards/:id/purchase` calls from `audit_buyer_v261` with `stock: 1` or coins sufficient for only 1 item must result in exactly 1 `201 Created` voucher and 1 `400 Bad Request` (`Reward out of stock` or `Not enough coins`), with coupons rolled back cleanly on the failed call.
- **Targeted Flash Sale (`targetRewards` non-empty) vs global Flash Sale (`targetRewards` empty):** When an active `ShopEvent` specifies `targetRewards: [rewardA._id]`, `rewardB` must remain at full price on Backend (`calculateDiscountedPrice`), Mobile shelf (`RewardCard`), and Mobile checkout modal (`RewardDetailModal`).
- **Expired voucher redemption guard (`expiresAt < now`):** Calling `PATCH /api/rewards/vouchers/:code/use` (User) or `PATCH /api/rewards/vouchers/:code/confirm` (Admin) on a voucher whose `expiresAt` is in the past must auto-mark or reject the voucher with `400 Voucher has expired` and never transition it to `pending_use` or `used`.
- **Unread approval notification lifecycle (`hasUnreadApproval`):** When Admin confirms a voucher (`status: 'used'`), `hasUnreadApproval` becomes `true`, `GET /api/rewards/vouchers/unread` returns that voucher to the user, and `PATCH /api/rewards/vouchers/:code/read` sets `hasUnreadApproval: false` so subsequent polls return `[]`.
- **Fragment exchange mapped via `SpecialItemConfig`:** When a user exchanges `requiredFragments` of a `SpecialItem` whose `type` (e.g. `frag_trasua` or `frag_50k`) matches an active `SpecialItemConfig`, the service atomically deducts the fragments and creates an active `Voucher` linked to the mapped `Reward` (with `fragmentType` snapshot).
